Tteokbokki

What is Tteokboki?

Tteokbokki is a popular Korean comfort food made from chewy, cylinder-shaped rice cakes cooked in a rich, sweet, and spicy sauce.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups of water
  • 2 dried anchovies
  • 3 tablespoons chili paste
  • 2 tablespoons white sugar
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon corn syrup
  • 2 korean fish cakes, sliced
  • 1/2 onion, thickly sliced
  • 1 spring onion, thickly sliced

Steps

  1. Combine water and anchovies in a saucepan; bring to a boil and cook for 10 minutes. Remove and discard anchovies; set anchovy water aside.
  2. Combine chili paste, sugar, soy sauce, and corn syrup in a bowl
  3. Add rice cakes, onion, and chili paste mixture to anchovy water; bring to a boil and boil for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally. Add spring onion; boil 3 minutes more.
  4. Serve hot and enjoy!
